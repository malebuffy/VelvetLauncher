# Velvet Launcher

Velvet Launcher brings a full web browser to your phone and Android Auto head unit so you can browse the web on the car display.

It is a launcher: it prepares what you need, then launches the browser when you ask. It does not ship a browser inside the app package.

## What it does

- Lets you download the components you choose to use, when you choose to use them
- Launches a web browser on your device
- Shows that browser on the Android Auto head unit so you can use it on the car screen
- Offers simple settings such as zoom, touch sensitivity, home page, and fullscreen start
- By default, blocks the display while the vehicle is moving for your protection (passenger use only if you explicitly enable **Display in Motion**)

## Safety and driving disclaimer

**Do not use the browser display while driving.**

Looking at a screen while the vehicle is in motion is distracting and may be illegal where you live. Velvet Launcher is intended for use when the vehicle is **safely parked**, or for a **passenger** display only where local law allows.

- By default, the display is blocked while in motion for your protection
- **Display in Motion** (allowing the display while moving) may conflict with local traffic laws
- You are solely responsible for complying with all applicable laws
- We strongly advise against enabling Display in Motion unless you are a passenger and the law permits it
- Display use should only happen while the vehicle is safely parked, unless you accept full responsibility for passenger use under local rules

Enabling Display in Motion requires an in-app acknowledgment of this responsibility. Enabling it does not make unsafe use legal or safe.
